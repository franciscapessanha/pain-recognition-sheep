Pain Estimation
==================================
* train_SVM.py: will train a SVM model for each fold
    - a nested cross validation was made to select the hyperparameters
    - the images from the same video are in the same group (test, validation or train)
    - all faces were flipped according to there absolute yaw value

* HOGs_features.py: collect the HOG features 

* geometric_features.py: collect the geometric features

Requirements
-------------------
* sklearn
* opencv-python
