Dataset structure
======================
* "data": full dataset divided intro the 4 subsets
    - farm
    - flickr
    - mixed
    - wsid

* "pose_lms_info": includes the faces oriented according to their absolute yaw and the pose and landmark information for each of the subsets

* "illness": videos from known ill sheep organized in folders according to their ID

* "scored": information regarding the pain score and the "first_frames" dataset with images extracted from short videos (one for each short 1 second clip)

* "video_annotations": 
    - clips: 1 second clips extracted from several videos
    - first_frames: first frames of each of this clips
    - pain: pain score associated
    - txt: beginning and end of each clip as well as a first annotation of pain score
